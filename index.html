The Two-Track Buy Box

§07 · Parallel Acquisition Frameworks
Track A · The Suburban Strategy

Suburban Premium

"Where families with disposable income spend their evenings and weekends."

Geography

  • Counties: Collin, Denton, NE Tarrant — >50% pop. growth since 2010.
  • Top submarkets: Allen (3.4% vac), McKinney (+3.5% absorb), Frisco/W. Frisco, N. Collin Cty Outlying (+10.8% absorb #1), Northwest FW (+2.5% absorb #4), Irving (2.0% vac #6).
  • Hard exclude: SW Dallas County (8.2% vac, –1.7% absorb #68), W. Fort Worth (9.2% vac).

Demographics & Schools

  • Median HHI ≥ $100k in ZIP (DFW metro = $95k).
  • 5-yr pop growth ≥ 1.0%; income forecast ≥ 3.5%.
  • Multiple GreatSchools 8+ within 2 miles.
  • A/B-rated ISDs: Frisco, Allen, Plano, Keller, Lewisville, GCISD, Coppell, Northwest.

Asset & Deal Profile

  • Size: 7,000–30,000 SF · multi-tenant strip / neighborhood / grocery-anchored.
  • Total ask: ≤ $11M — first-acquisition equity check sizing.
  • Tenant mix: Daily-needs / service / F&B (fitness, medical, restaurant).
  • Vintage post-1995; CoStar Star Rating ≥ 3.
  • Cap 6.25–7.5%; price/SF below subtype avg ($284 Strip, $220 Nbhd).
Track B · The Urban Strategy

Urban Infill & Redevelopment

"Where daytime population, walkability, and district momentum drive the deal."

Target Districts

  • Fort Worth: Near Southside / Magnolia / Medical District, West 7th, Cultural District.
  • Dallas: Bishop Arts / N Oak Cliff (75208), Lakewood / Lower Greenville / Knox-Henderson (75204/75206), Deep Ellum, Trinity Groves.
  • Common thread: active redevelopment, walkable street grid, mixed-use density.

Population & Anchors

  • 3-mi population ≥ 100k (density over residential HHI).
  • Daytime employment anchor — medical district, university, employment hub.
  • Walkability ≥ 80; Walk-Score-style street grid.
  • Schools waived — F&B / lifestyle tenants don't recruit on school zones.

Asset & Deal Profile

  • Size: 7,000–30,000 SF · multi-tenant strip / street-front retail.
  • Total ask: ≤ $11M — first-acquisition equity check sizing.
  • Type: Restaurant / F&B / lifestyle / experiential, recently built or gut-renovated (post-2020 ideal); CoStar Star ≥ 3.
  • Cap 5.5–7.0% tolerated (urban infill premium); price/SF can exceed subtype avg if district rent growth supports.
  • WALT ≥ 4 years; watch tenant rollover concentration carefully.

Data Sources & Methodology

  • Our scoring framework — buy-box criteria combined with FrontView REIT's net-lease strategy: two parallel 1–10 scores — Real Estate (Traffic & Frontage 25% · Demographics & MSA 15% · E-Commerce Resistance 15% · Re-Tenantability 20% · Tenant Diversity 10% · Value Creation 15%) and Tenant & Lease (Credit 30% · Lease Structure 15% · WALT 20% · Escalators 15% · Renewal Options 5% · Rent Coverage 15%) — blended 60% RE / 40% Tenant. Weights are adjustable live in the Investment Thesis panel (§02.5); all scores, decisions, the ranking and the map recompute on change.
  • Hard gates (auto NO-GO): outside 7,000–30,000 SF or ask > $11M · traffic < 10,000 VPD · trade-area population < 30,000 (10-mi) · specialized single-use building · WALT < 3 yr. Pricing is handled by the decision ladder, not the gates: a non-IG asking cap more than 50 bps below the model-implied fair cap reads PASS (Overpriced) and the asset keeps its fundamentals rank.
  • Recommended cap band & fair value: cap (%) = MAX(5.5, 10.5 − blended × 0.525) ± 25 bps; implied fair value = NOI ÷ recommended cap. Benchmarked to FrontView REIT's Q1 2026 actuals — TTM acquisition cap 7.59%, disposition cap 6.79%, +80 bps spread.
  • Pipeline (54 deals): 35 with verified Offering Memoranda in the OM folder (click any GO/CAUTION deal to open its OM); 19 from CoStar / LoopNet exports (May 2026), flagged "No OM on file." Size / check-size filter: 7,000–30,000 SF · ≤ $11M ask.
  • Submarket fundamentals (rent, vacancy, 12-mo absorption, growth) from CoStar's Q2 2026 DFW Retail Market Report, covering 69 DFW submarkets / 470M SF — applied consistently per submarket. Demographics from OM radius reports where available, otherwise US Census ACS 2024 ZIP-level estimates.
  • Data confidence: the four real-estate judgment factors (e-commerce resistance, re-tenantability, tenant diversity, value creation), plus traffic and 10-mi trade-area population where the OM is silent, are analyst estimates — strongest for the 35 OM-verified deals, directional for the 19 CoStar/LoopNet listings. This is a top-of-funnel screen, not a substitute for deal-level underwriting (DCF / IRR with debt, Texas tax reassessment, lease-by-lease rollover) and confirmatory diligence (T-12s, PCA, environmental, title, zoning, estoppels).
